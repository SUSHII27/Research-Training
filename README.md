# 🔬 Research Experience

## 1️⃣ CSIR – Indian Institute of Toxicology Research (CSIR-IITR), Lucknow

### Research Project

**Graphene Oxide–Protein Interaction Studies for Biosensing Applications**

### Duration

**January 2023 – May 2023**

### Supervisors

- **Dr. Rachana Kumar** – Principal Scientist
- **Dr. V. P. Sharma** – Chief Scientist

### Overview

Conducted hands-on research investigating the interaction between graphene oxide and proteins for biosensing applications. The project focused on understanding protein adsorption behaviour and evaluating graphene oxide as a potential biosensing material.

### Responsibilities

- Conducted experimental laboratory work
- Assisted in graphene oxide–protein interaction studies
- Performed scientific observations and data interpretation
- Maintained laboratory documentation
- Participated in scientific discussions and literature review

### Techniques & Exposure

- Protein interaction studies
- Nanomaterial characterization
- UV–Visible Spectroscopy
- Laboratory experimentation
- Scientific data interpretation
- Biosensor research concepts

### Outcome

✅ Research contributed to a peer-reviewed scientific publication in **Carbon Trends (2024).**

### Skills Developed

- Experimental Research
- Scientific Documentation
- Literature Review
- Data Interpretation
- Scientific Writing
- Laboratory Practices
